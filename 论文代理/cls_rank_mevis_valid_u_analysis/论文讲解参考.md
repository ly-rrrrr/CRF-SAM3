论文里的简洁分析段落
中文版
为分析 Phase-2 verifier 的作用，我们在 MeViS valid_u 的 candidate sets 上比较了 base 与 finetuned 模型的候选判别与排序行为。
如图 X 所示，训练后正候选的 P(Yes) 明显升高，而负候选的 P(Yes) 明显降低，说明 L_cls 有效增强了正负候选的可分性。具体而言，正候选的平均 P(Yes) 由 0.4857 提升至 0.7685，负候选则由 0.4558 降至 0.1917。
同时，训练后同一 expression 下最强正候选相对于最强负候选的 logit gap 由 -1.3625 提升至 4.1788，表明 L_rank 显著增强了正候选的排序优势。对应地，top-1 为正候选的比例由 37% 提升至 78%，且 47% 的 expression 从原先 top-1 错误翻转为 top-1 正确。
这些结果表明，该模块不仅提升了 candidate-level 的二分类判别能力，也显著改善了候选集合内的相对排序质量。

英文版
To analyze the effect of the Phase-2 verifier, we compare the candidate-level prediction and ranking behavior of the base and finetuned models on MeViS valid_u candidate sets.
As shown in Fig. X, after training, the verifier assigns substantially higher P(Yes) to positive candidates and lower P(Yes) to negative candidates, indicating that L_cls improves positive-negative separability. Concretely, the mean P(Yes) for positive candidates increases from 0.4857 to 0.7685, while that for negative candidates decreases from 0.4558 to 0.1917.
Meanwhile, the logit gap between the strongest positive and strongest negative candidate within each expression increases from -1.3625 to 4.1788, showing that L_rank significantly strengthens the relative ordering of positive candidates. Correspondingly, the top-1 positive rate improves from 37% to 78%, and 47% of expressions are corrected from an incorrect top-1 candidate to a correct one.
These results show that the proposed module improves not only candidate-level binary discrimination but also ranking quality within each candidate set.
