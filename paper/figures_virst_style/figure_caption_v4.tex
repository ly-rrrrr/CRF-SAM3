\caption{\textbf{Overview of CRF-SAM3.}
\textbf{(a)} Inference. The \emph{Ground} stage forms a compact recall concept $\hat c$ from RGB frames and the expression $q$; SAM3 \emph{recalls} candidate tubes $\mathcal{C}(q)$; the \emph{Verify} stage scores each tube against the full expression, and the top-$\hat N$ tubes, with $\hat N$ given by a counting prompt, are merged into the prediction $Y$. No mask is used at inference.
\textbf{(b)} Ground training. Category-agnostic queries attend to the expression features $T$ and the projected visual tokens $\bar V$ and produce token responses $R$. The best-matching slot $k^{*}$ is supervised by the token-level GT mask ($\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{ground}}$); at test time responses are aggregated by $\max_k$. The resulting gate softly modulates the original visual tokens $V$ (and the DeepStack features) without cropping or pruning, and the frozen decoder generates $\hat c$, supervised by $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{cat}}$.
\textbf{(c)} Verify training. Candidates are cached offline and labelled by IoU with the GT tube; no gradient flows through SAM3. The LoRA-adapted verifier scores each mask-marked candidate with $s_i=P(\mathrm{Yes}\mid q,\tau_i)$ and is trained with listwise competition on $s$ ($\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{list}}$), a hard-negative margin on the logit gap $r$ ($\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{hard}}$), and mask-aware alignment of its hidden states ($\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{align}}$).}
